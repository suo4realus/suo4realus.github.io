Best move

Play moves or set up any position. Your coach finds the strongest move, explains the idea behind it, and reviews every move you play.

Set up position

Pick a piece, then tap squares to place it. Tap a square again to remove it.

Read a board from a picture

Copy a screenshot and tap Paste picture, or choose one from your photos. Screenshots from chess apps work best. The reading happens on your device.

The yellow grid marks the board. If it doesn't line up with the squares, drag from one corner of the board to the opposite corner to redraw it. It snaps onto the squares automatically.

Train it on your chess app (one time)
  1. In your chess app, start a new game and take a screenshot before anyone moves.
  2. Choose that screenshot above with Choose picture and check the yellow grid fits the board.
  3. Tap Learn my pieces.

After that, screenshots from the same app and piece style are read using your own pieces as the reference. If you change the board theme or piece style, train it again.
